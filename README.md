# The Shawshank Redemption

> "Get busy living, or get busy dying."

**The Shawshank Redemption** is a 1994 drama film directed by Frank Darabont. It follows Andy Dufresne, a man wrongly imprisoned for murder, and his friendship with Ellis "Red" Redding.

## Why I Love It
* Incredible story about hope and resilience
* Amazing acting performances
* One of the best movie endings ever

## Main Characters
* **Andy Dufresne** - The smart, hopeful banker
* **Red Redding** - The prison contraband smuggler and Andy's best friend
